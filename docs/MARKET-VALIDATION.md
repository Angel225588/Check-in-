# Market validation — "Check-in" breakfast PWA (round-table run #1)

*Method: 5 specialist agents (demand · competitors · pricing · market size · go-to-market),
each doing cited desk research, synthesized below. Re-run this per idea — see the
framework at the bottom.*

## Verdict: **GO (4/5)** — real wedge, thin competition, modest French TAM

Sell to **independent Paris hotels first**, launch-test at **€49/property/month**, and
lead with **founder walk-ins + a filmed Courtyard reference**. The risk is not demand
— it's that the moat is distribution/workflow, not technology, and France alone is a
modest market, so plan land-and-expand beyond France for a big outcome.

---

## The five lenses

**1. Demand — strong, daily, and real.** The pain is *entitlement-vs-covers
reconciliation* (who's included/comp vs who showed up) + door speed. Felt hardest by
F&B and reception managers, lived by the breakfast supervisor. **Independents 4/5 vs
franchises 3/5** — same pain, but the independent owner/GM decides and pays without
brand approval. 70–80% staff turnover ⇒ the UI must be near-zero-training, FR/EN,
offline-tolerant.

**2. Competition — near-empty category.** No mainstream product runs the breakfast
*door*. PMS (Opera, Mews, Cloudbeds) and POS (Simphony, Lightspeed) only *bill*
breakfast via package accounting — they don't do "search a room, tap to check in."
The real default is **paper printout + highlighter**. Low technical moat (OCR is
commodity); defensibility = distribution, workflow lock-in, and accumulated
report-format/VIP-matching logic — not the code.

**3. Pricing — €49/mo launch.** Comparable small-hotel ops add-ons run €40–100/mo.
Value protected is ~€700–1,800/mo (recovered staff time + comp-leakage + food waste),
so €49 is a 15–40× value/price no-brainer.
- **Solo €49/mo** (≤30 rooms) · **Pro €69/mo** (30–80 rooms, adds payment/comp +
  no-show analytics) · **Group €59/property** (franchise multi-site).
- 30-day free **trial** (not freemium). OCR cost ≈ **€0.5/property/month** ⇒ **~95%+
  gross margin**.

**4. Market size — modest in France, expand later.** ~13,500–15,000 French hotels
(~75% independent by count); Île-de-France ~2,455; **Paris ~1,626, ~900–1,400
independent**. Nearly all serve breakfast.
- **France TAM ≈ €13M ARR** (€8–17M range) · **IDF SAM ≈ €2M** · **Paris-independent
  beachhead ≈ €0.9M ARR.** Small ⇒ treat France as the proving base, plan multi-country.

**5. Go-to-market — proximity + proof.** Beachhead = **independent Paris 3-star hotels**
(dense, walkable, owner-buyer, no brand gatekeeping).
- **Channels by speed:** walk-ins ≈ referrals > LinkedIn founder outreach > cold
  call/email > associations (**GNI** = the independent-hotel body) > **EquipHotel Paris
  (2–5 Nov 2026)** > PMS marketplaces.
- **Lead-gen (do, but don't over-buy):** Google Maps scrape (Outscraper/Apify) for the
  ~1,400-hotel list + **Lusha** (better EU coverage, GDPR/ISO) for decision-maker
  contacts + 1 Sales Navigator seat. **No lead-gen MCP/automation yet** — the list is a
  one-time ~1,400 rows; spend energy on demos, not plumbing.
- **Content:** LinkedIn primary, Instagram secondary, **skip TikTok**. Phone-shot proof,
  not polished ads. 2–3 posts/week.

### Highest-leverage first move
**Film the "6am breakfast door — paper vs one-tap" before/after clip + a 60-sec Courtyard
manager testimonial, then walk independent hotels in the arrondissements around that
Courtyard, phone in hand, 30-day pilot on offer.** Proof + proximity + founder-led pilots
= fastest path to the first 5 paying hotels (~8–10 weeks).

## Top risks
- **Low tech moat** — a PMS/POS could add "door mode." Mitigate with distribution speed +
  workflow lock-in + report-format/VIP logic.
- **Modest French TAM** — need a multi-country plan for a venture-scale outcome.
- **GDPR** on cold outreach (B2B, state source + opt-out; Lusha helps, obligation is yours).
